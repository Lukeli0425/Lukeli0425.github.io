## Abstract

Strong gravitational lensing reveals important information about the universe, but reconstructing the source image and lens properties requires solving an ill-posed inverse imaging problem.
Generative image models serve as powerful data-driven priors in Bayesian inverse problems.
Diffusion models have been used widely, but they are computationally costly.
Flow matching has emerged as a promising alternative to diffusion models, with the advantage of straighter probability paths that enable easier training, efficient sampling, and better-quality data generation.
In this work, we present **Blind-FlowSGS**, a principled blind posterior sampling method using flow matching priors, enabling efficient posterior sampling for non-blind and blind inversion of strong gravitational lenses.
On simulated Euclid data, we demonstrate our method's improvement over diffusion-based methods in both efficacy and efficiency.
Our method shows promising capabilities to accurately process strong lensing data from modern sky surveys at scale.
